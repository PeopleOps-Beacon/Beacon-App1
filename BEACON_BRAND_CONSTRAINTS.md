# Beacon Brand Constraints

These elements are fixed constraints for Beacon interface refinements:

- Product name: Beacon.
- Logo system: existing Beacon full logo and mark assets in `public/brand/`.
- Tagline: "Navigate the airport with clear directions built around your needs."
- Brand palette: deep black and charcoal backgrounds, warm gold primary accents, cream text, and subtle amber glow treatments.
- Typography: existing app typography and weight system.
- Icon language: clean line icons paired with gold or cream treatments.
- Background style: premium dark surfaces, subtle airport-grid structure, gold lighting, and restrained depth.
- Personality: trustworthy, calm, modern, accessible, premium, and passenger-focused.

Interface work should improve usability, accessibility, spacing, navigation, hierarchy, and clarity without replacing, recoloring, or reinterpreting the Beacon identity.
