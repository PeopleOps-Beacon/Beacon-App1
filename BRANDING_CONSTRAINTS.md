# Beacon Branding Constraints

These elements are fixed constraints for Beacon interface work. Product refinements may improve usability, spacing, hierarchy, accessibility, and clarity, but must not reinterpret or replace the established brand.

## Fixed Brand Elements

- **Logo:** Existing Beacon lighthouse mark and full Beacon logo from `public/brand/` and `src/brand/brandAssets.ts`.
- **Name:** Beacon.
- **Tagline:** Navigation designed around the traveler.
- **Primary colors:** Beacon black `#030405`, graphite `#111315` / `#11151c`, and warm white text.
- **Accent colors:** Beacon gold `#f5b72f`, bright amber `#ffcf66` / `#ffd96d`, and deep gold `#9d6516`.
- **Neutral support:** Brand steel `#636763` and muted slate/steel text treatments already used in the app.
- **Typography:** Existing Inter/system UI stack.
- **Icon style:** Existing lucide icon style with simple line icons.
- **Border radius:** Compact premium radius of `8px` for cards, buttons, inputs, map panels, and dialogs; pill radius only for status chips and bottom navigation items that need a clear touch shape.
- **Button styling:** Beacon gold primary actions with dark text; dark translucent secondary actions with subtle gold borders; icon-plus-label controls for navigation and operational actions.
- **Spacing system:** Mobile-first spacing built around 8px increments, with 12px, 16px, 20px, 24px, and 32px as common layout steps.
- **Shadow system:** Restrained dark elevation using soft shadows such as `0 18px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.42)`; avoid heavy glow except for minimal gold emphasis on active route or brand mark.
- **Background treatment:** Existing dark graphite surface, subtle grid, and restrained gold halo/glow treatments.
- **Card styles:** Dark charcoal surfaces with warm white text, low-contrast borders, consistent padding, and clear single-purpose grouping.
- **Personality:** Premium, trustworthy, modern, calm, accessibility-forward, and airport-appropriate.

## Redesign Rules

- Do not create, crop, recolor, stretch, or replace the Beacon logo.
- Do not introduce competing palettes or generic airport colors.
- Do not replace the existing typography or icon family unless required for a narrow accessibility fix.
- Keep meaningful branded elements visible while simplifying.
- Use Beacon gold and amber treatments consistently for emphasis, progress, and primary action.
- Prefer spacing, hierarchy, clearer copy, and reduced visual density over new brand concepts.
- If accessibility requires adjustment, preserve the brand as closely as possible and make the smallest necessary contrast or readability change.
