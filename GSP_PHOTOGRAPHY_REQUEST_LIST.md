# GSP Photography Request List

This shot list is for GSP communications or an approved airport photographer. Requested images should be reviewed for privacy, security, currency, and Beacon demonstration use before integration.

| Location | Why Beacon needs it | Camera position | Direction of view | Primary landmark | Framing | Preferred time | Passengers | Minimum resolution | Orientation | Accessibility relevance | Security review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main entrance doors | Door-level preview before the traveler enters the terminal. | About 25 feet from the automatic doors at traveler eye level. | Facing the entry doors from the curbside approach. | Main terminal entrance doors and overhead entrance signage. | 4:3 crop with doors occupying about half the frame and curb/walkway context visible. | Bright daytime with low glare on glass. | Minimal background passengers; avoid recognizable faces. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show step-free curb-to-door approach. | Standard public exterior review. |
| Ticketing lobby and airline counters | Preview before travelers identify their airline check-in area. | Traveler eye level near the entrance side of the ticketing lobby. | Facing the ticket counter row with queue lanes visible. | Ticketing counter row and overhead wayfinding signs. | 4:3, counters occupying the middle half of the image. | Normal operating hours. | Minimal or blurred background passengers. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show open queue lanes and step-free approach. | Review airline branding, passenger privacy, and visible documents. |
| Standard security and TSA PreCheck approaches | Preview before choosing the correct security lane approach. | Public pre-security path, outside sensitive screening detail. | Facing public lane entrance signs only. | Public security lane entrance signage. | 4:3, entrance signage visible, no screening interfaces or restricted areas. | Normal operating hours if approved. | Avoid recognizable faces and documents. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show open approach path and accessible lane context where appropriate. | Mandatory GSP security review. |
| Concourse A/B entrances and split | Preview before travelers choose the correct concourse direction. | Post-security traveler path before the split. | Facing overhead concourse signs and the two path choices. | Concourse A and Concourse B signs. | 4:3 with both paths visible and signs readable. | Normal operating hours with minimal crowding. | Minimal background passengers. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show wide step-free circulation path. | Review to avoid sensitive security details behind photographer. |
| Gate podium and seating | Arrival preview for gate confirmation. | Traveler eye level 20-30 feet from a representative gate podium. | Facing podium, boarding doorway, and adjacent seating. | Gate podium and gate sign. | 4:3, podium and gate sign large enough to recognize. | Outside boarding peaks. | Avoid close faces and boarding documents. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show step-free approach and accessible seating context. | Review airline signage, passenger privacy, and gate operations. |
| Baggage claim and ground transportation path | Arrival route preview from baggage claim to pickup areas. | Traveler eye level near carousel and exit path. | Facing carousel or ground transportation exit signs. | Carousel, baggage claim sign, or pickup exit sign. | 4:3 with one primary landmark and open route path. | Normal arrivals period with low crowd density. | Minimal or blurred background passengers; avoid luggage tags. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show clear floor space around carousel or exit. | Review passenger privacy and any operational screens. |
| Parking, rideshare, rental cars, and assistance points | Preview transportation and support destinations currently simulated. | Traveler eye level from public approach path to each service point. | Facing the service landmark with route continuing beyond when relevant. | Service sign, desk, counter, pickup zone, or covered walkway. | 4:3, service point occupying about half the visual. | Bright public operating conditions. | Minimal passengers; avoid faces, plates, IDs, and documents. | 2400 x 1800 | Landscape | Show curb cuts, accessible counter access, or step-free route context. | Review restricted doors, credentials, screens, plates, and privacy. |
